1. Your headline should explain.
   “Reimagining digital experiences” sounds nice. It also says nothing.
   Visitors should understand what you do in 3 seconds.
2. One page = one goal.
   If you’re asking users to sign up, don’t also ask them to download a PDF, read a blog, and follow you on social media.
3. Above-the-fold is not for decoration.
   Hero images, abstract shapes, and empty space don’t sell.
   A strong value proposition + CTA does

4. Show the product early.
   People don’t trust concepts.
   They trust interfaces, dashboards, and real screenshots.
5. Your CTA should be specific.
   “Get Started” is weak.
   “Start free 14-day trial” tells users exactly what happens next.
6. Social proof should reduce risk.
   Logos are fine. But testimonials that mention real results convert better.

7. Remove anything that makes users think.
   Too many sections, animations, or unclear icons slow decision-making.
   Clarity beats creativity.
8. Your page should answer objections before users ask.
   Pricing, integrations, security, and support :hiding these lowers trust.
9. Speed is part of design.
   A beautiful page that loads in 4 seconds is a bad page.
10. Design hierarchy should guide the eye.
    If everything is bold, colorful, and animated, nothing is important.
