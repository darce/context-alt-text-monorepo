# Caption quality and occlusion model-combination assessment

**Assessment date:** 2026-10-07
**Scope:** Remote-only review of caption selection, occlusion-aware face/head evidence, and candidate model combinations in this checkout.
**Decision status:** Research recommendation only. No production change, model promotion, measured quality gain, or speed gain is claimed.

## Recommendation

Keep the repository's pinned **Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B-Instruct Q4_K_M** as the caption comparator. Treat its raw single-view profile and existing asynchronous four-view ensemble as distinct baselines. Evaluate additions in a frozen, offline sequence: (1) compare the existing caption selector with a candidate-level semantic ranker, (2) test bounded, unmarked person/head views with source-coordinate grounding, (3) test numbered markers only as a controlled auxiliary view, and (4) compare **Qwen3.8-27B** on the same arms. Keep EmbeddingGemma 2 on the caption-retrieval/ranking side; it is not a caption generator, a face recognizer, a visibility estimator, or proof that a caption is true.

For occluded identity, first measure whether a real visible face cue is available and whether the detector found it. A pose-derived ROI may improve localization; it cannot restore a cue hidden by sunglasses or a mask. Qwen may describe a visibly supported person, clothing, or accessory, but it must not guess a name or reconstruct hidden facial traits. Keep whole-face, periocular, head, body, and image-text semantic representations in separate comparison contracts. Clothing or whole-image similarity can at most support provisional, bounded within-event review; an upload batch alone is not an event, and appearance vectors must not update persistent face centroids. Preserve the current unknown/abstain and roster-grounded naming rules. No branch is ready for activation on the evidence reviewed here.

I decline to forecast a repository effect size. The checked-in materials do not provide a matched, blind human-caption baseline for these proposed arms, and the existing occlusion labels are image-level rather than face-level. The values below are experiment gates, not predicted outcomes.

## Proposed diagnostic sequence

Each stage uses a frozen source image set, prompt, token budget, serving hardware and baseline outputs. Change one factor at a time and retain a full-image answer for every arm.

| Stage | Controlled comparison | Primary question and stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Freeze baselines | Raw single-view Qwen3-VL vs the current four-view lexical selector, with identical view generation and outputs retained. | Establish paired, source-hashed candidates. No new quality claim until blind adjudication exists. |
| 1. Candidate-level semantic ranker | Re-rank those same captions with word-Jaccard and an EmbeddingGemma 2 arm. Encode original image and unmarked ROIs separately from candidate text; use the model's documented task formatting and image-text space. Score explicit visible-fact support and view consistency, and preserve a scene-level coverage check. | Tests ranking only; it adds embedding work to a one-pass request. Similarity is neither truth nor a probability. Include unsupported but fluent candidate captions and image/crop counterfactuals. Stop if shared hallucinations rank higher, scene content is lost, or person binding becomes worse. |
| 2. Unmarked person/head ROIs | One full-scene view plus zero to two bounded, unmarked head/person crops. Compare full image, person box, predicted pose proposals, and oracle head boxes. Use reliable visible COCO-style head landmarks where present; otherwise use calibrated shoulder/body-axis geometry with uncertainty, not a fixed head-height fraction. If face recognition is evaluated on a crop, rerun the native detector on the crop, map detections through the crop transform to source coordinates, and deduplicate. | Separates localization from conditional recognition. If oracle head boxes do not improve crop-conditional identity, better pose proposals alone should not be expected to improve identity; caption detail and localization utility remain separate outcomes. If predicted proposals add calls without true recovered faces or accepted caption facts, stop. No landmark filled through an occluder is marked observed. |
| 3. Marker auxiliary view | Add a SoM-like numbered overlay only after the unmarked ROI arm. Keep original pixels authoritative; use fixed total views/tokens and include a marker-only non-occluding control, shuffled/misplaced marker, and blank/wrong-ROI negative. | Tests reference correspondence, not hidden semantic recovery or guaranteed model attention. Stop if marker pixels leak into generated facts, face cues are covered, or the result is indistinguishable from a larger unmarked crop. |
| 4. Visibility specialist or mask assist | Only after stages 1–2 demonstrate a relevant gain, test human-verified versus predicted masks and a separately trained region/periocular matcher with its own gallery, embedding space and preprocessing. Calibrate any 1:1 verification FAR/FMR diagnostic separately from the open-set identification policy. | The periocular branch is a hypothesis for lower-face masks when eyes remain visible; it is invalid evidence under opaque sunglasses. S³POT-style generated reference pixels may suggest a segmentation mask, but never enter enrollment, identity embeddings, or emitted visible facts. Stop when mask errors suppress visible identity pixels or downstream recognition does not improve. |
| 5. Generator challenger | Run Qwen3.8-27B UD-Q4_K_XL on the same preregistered arms only after the Qwen3-VL baseline is frozen. Validate its runtime/build and no-think behavior, and report quality, latency, VRAM and failures separately. A higher-precision or different-quant arm must use the same model family and recipe. | Isolates model/quantization from crop, marker, and ranker changes. Stop on critical-fact or person-binding regression, unsupported runtime behavior, or cost outside the declared budget. No quantization conclusion from unrelated general-knowledge tests. |

Typed ROI/fact evidence should retain the source-image hash, person instance, source-coordinate box, crop transform, proposer/model revision, keypoint confidence and *visibility* separately, coverage/uncertainty, view ID, and the fact/caption source. A model-filled landmark under an occluder is a proposal, not an observation. A caption selected from a crop must be grounded against the original scene before source-coordinate phrase boxes or named-person attachments are reused.

```text
original image ──┬── full-scene Qwen baseline ─────────────┐
                 ├── bounded unmarked ROI views ───────────┤
                 ├── optional numbered auxiliary view ─────┤── candidate captions
                 └── source-coordinate / person mapping ───┘          │
                                                                      ▼
                                            text/image ranker + abstain/grounding gate
                                                                      │
                                                                      ▼
                                      caption facts → existing roster and consent checks
```

The ranker must not compare an image-plus-caption interleaved input with that same caption as if this were independent confirmation. Prefer separate image-only and text-only vectors, explicit source-view/person indices, counterfactual negatives and human adjudication. A closed fact ledger can identify the supported and unsupported nouns/actions/accessories/person references in each caption; it does not turn cosine similarity into a truth detector.

## Evaluation contract and proposed adoption gate

1. **Build the face-level gold set before scoring.** Include real clean, mask, sunglasses, both, other occluders, profile, tiny head, overlapping people, blur and out-of-domain cases. Annotate every face/person instance independently of proposals: source-coordinate face/head/person boxes, identity or explicit unknown, person↔head↔face links, occluder region/type, visible/occluded/self-occluded/out-of-frame/uncertain state by anatomical landmark, and annotator disagreement. Keep consent scope explicit for evaluation and for any later training. Group by identity, capture event/session, duplicate source and synthetic descendant before split. Preserve a deployment-probability sample alongside an enriched challenge set.
2. **Treat historic tags as mining hints only.** The existing 646-image inventory has 591 consent-on-file personal images and 55 celebrity fixtures; the old hand labels cover only 150 images and are image-level. Historical counts of 13 sunglasses, 5 mask, and 19 other-occlusion positives are too small and too weakly attributed to establish face-level condition coverage. The prior 13/13 or 5/5 caption-keyword retrieval figures are shortlist recall against old image tags, not true occlusion recall or caption precision. Recheck the current manifest and image files before acquisition; the August finding that 449 images had no captions is not a current readiness statement.
3. **Adjudicate captions blind and paired.** Score supported specificity, scene/action completeness, accessories and relations; unsupported object/person/name/location/intent/emotion/cause; crop coverage; and which person each phrase describes. “Face mask” may be visible; COVID intent is not. Include an unknown/abstain choice. Separate no-context, authorized-context and decoy-context cases, and never award a context echo as a visual fact.
4. **Measure the failure stages separately.** Report face/head recall at matched FPPI, localization and person-association precision/recall, crop-conditional recognition, end-to-end correct names with missed detections in the denominator, and unknown rate. Reuse the existing [`detection_pr_strict`](../../../apps/prototype-description-service/scripts/eval_harness/face_metrics.py#L782) contract and [`associate_detections`](../../../apps/prototype-description-service/scripts/eval_harness/face_assignment.py#L215) assignment; strict detection scoring already requires exhaustive annotations, independently supported and human-adjudicated GT, and the IoU threshold in the run manifest. Do not rebuild these metrics. The end-to-end open-set identification gate is FNIR at a ratified fixed FPIR, with the gallery size and non-mated population named and the threshold fixed from calibration before sealed-test scoring. The current [`fnir_fpi_at_threshold`](../../../apps/prototype-description-service/scripts/eval_harness/open_set_identification.py#L128) diagnostic reports FNIR at a fixed score threshold and FPI as an integer count; FPI is not an FPIR rate and must not be silently normalized or substituted for that gate. FAR/FMR belongs to a separately labeled 1:1 verification diagnostic with its own threshold and trial population. Compare corresponding visible support on both probe and gallery for partial-face recognition; do not relax a false-accept limit to buy recall. The rule of three (about 30,000 independent negative trials for a 10⁻⁴ zero-error bound) applies to an appropriately independent 1:1 pair-FMR diagnostic, not automatically to correlated 1:N searches.
5. **Record service cost end to end.** Measure p50/p95/p99 for proposal, embedding, generation and grounding, queue and warm/cold paths, cache hit rates, peak VRAM, GPU cost per accepted caption, failures/timeouts, and fraction escalated to multiview. Embedding encoding is not a free cosine operation; cache hits do not count as fast model inference. Four generation calls falling to two means two fewer calls arithmetically, not a measured 2× speedup.

**Proposed, not approved, adoption gate:** seek at least a 5 percentage-point paired gain in human-accepted captions and non-inferiority on preregistered critical factuality and person-binding errors, with same pins/hardware, group-aware intervals or design-effect sizing, a declared compute budget, and successful negative perturbation tests. This threshold is a decision proposal, not a forecast. Do not claim a gain until the paired run is adequately powered and the margin/holdout design was fixed before inspecting outcomes.

| Kill condition | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Shared unsupported details rise after ranker selection | Similarity is reinforcing correlated hallucinations, not verifying them. |
| Cross-person name or phrase binding increases | Crop/person evidence does not satisfy the existing one-to-one grounding boundary. |
| Marker, inpainted, or generated face texture appears as a visible fact | Artificial pixels crossed into the evidence contract. |
| Oracle ROI provides no conditional improvement | Pose-proposal engineering cannot repair the downstream missing-cue problem. |
| Proposal overhead yields no accepted-fact or localization benefit | Keep the extra routing work out of the request path. |
| Quant/model challenger regresses a critical slice or breaks runtime/template contract | Do not promote it based on model size, aggregate benchmark scores, or a different task. |
