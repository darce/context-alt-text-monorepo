All three test files enter the new environment manifest preflight while their transaction harnesses stub the older preflight steps. `preflight_env_manifest` defaults to enabled and launches `scripts/env/materialize_remote.sh` as a child process (`scripts/deploy/recognition-service.sh:989-1017`); deploy and promote call it at `:4835` and `:5004`. The child process does not inherit a shell-local `ssh()` function, so the tests hit real SSH before reaching their assertions. The two controlled recognition runs establish that the 49 failing transaction assertions themselves pass when the test-only external boundary is skipped.

The OCIR transaction-order case also needs a complete lease setup: it opts into remote build at `scripts/deploy/tests/test_recognition_ocir_config.py:548` but leaves the TTL at 7200 and does not stub `deploy_env_lease` (`:546-563`). The documented defaults compute a 7225-second floor for that remote-build budget (`scripts/deploy/recognition-service.sh:116-119,2214-2249`). This is a fixture configuration defect, not evidence against the transaction-order assertion.
