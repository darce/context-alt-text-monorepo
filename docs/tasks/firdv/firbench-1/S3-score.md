## Goal

Turn per-face search records into the frozen-τ answer: FNIR at the declared FPIR on a fixed non-mated source-unit denominator, end to end (EVAL-16, EVAL-18, EVAL-19). Produce paired PSU-cluster intervals, the sealed step-down decision, guardrails, and a verdict that can be "inconclusive" (CARD-27).

## Build

1. `finish_outcomes(records: Sequence[SearchRecord], tau: float, boundary: BoundarySemantics) -> tuple[FaceOutcome, ...]` (S0 Build 10). A record is a mated GT face or a non-mated record (a non-mated GT face or a background detection). A record with `arm_availability` other than `available` is not an outcome: it is excluded and counted per arm. A fused-leg record with stage `detected` (no embedding) finishes as `missed`; its stage stays `detected` in the waterfall.
   - `legacy_v1`: a non-mated record is accepted iff score > τ; a mated record iff score ≥ τ.
   - `v2`: accept iff score ≥ τ for both.
   - The semantics are recorded in the output.
2. `curve(records, *, units, thresholds, boundary) -> tuple[CurvePoint, ...]`: FNIR over mated GT faces, and FPI and FPIR over non-mated source units.
   - A unit is a false-accept unit at τ when any of its non-mated records is `false_accept`.
   - FPI = the number of false-accept units. It is an integer and counts units, not searches.
   - N_units is the fixed count from S1 `build_nonmated_pool` for the scored pool. It is never a detector count. Detector alarms never add units or searches to the denominator (EVAL-19; MCP 16735).
   - FPIR = FPI / N_units. It is at most 1 by construction.
   - Additional spurious names are reported separately as an integer. They are every above-threshold non-mated name beyond the first in a unit, and every above-threshold name on a duplicate detection over a mated face (S2 Build 5). They are reported per 1,000 frame images in their own field. They never enter FPI or FPIR.
   - Per-leg `pair_fmr` is `{status, reason, data}`. Explicit complete 1:1 scores alone give `available`, null reason and `data: {false_matches: int, n_nonmated_pairs: int, rate: number}`. Without them: `not_evaluated`, `pair_scores_not_supplied`, null data. Missing calibration or no nonmated pairs gives `unavailable`, a named reason and null data. Never derive FMR from truncated top-k searches or invent zero (MCP 16810).
   - Primary verdict, guardrails and FPIR use native probability records in the single primary test fold only; complementary folds fit τ. Challenge records remain separate descriptive outputs with `::sampling=challenge` leg keys, never confirmatory inputs (S2 Build 2).
3. `calibrate_tau(records: Sequence[SearchRecord], *, units: Sequence[NonMatedUnitLike], calibration_folds: frozenset[int], fpir_target: float, boundary: BoundarySemantics, identity_folds: Mapping[str, int] | None = None) -> float`:
   - Uses only units and records from calibration folds (CAL-07, EXP-22; MCP 16733). Any test-fold unit or test-fold probe raises `CalibrationLeakError`. Any top-k gallery identity whose fold is not in `calibration_folds` raises `CalibrationLeakError`, which mirrors `select_fit_trials`.
   - Fold provenance (MCP 16810): the file scorer passes the full predeclared `identity_folds` map from `gallery_membership`, covering query and enrolled identities, including enrolled identities with no mated probe. Every used probe with non-null `identity_id` must map to its record's fold; every used top-k identity must map to a calibration fold. A hit may belong to a different fit fold from its probe. Unused test-fold entries in the full map are allowed; using one is a leak. Fold membership alone never implies enrollment.
   - With `identity_folds=None`, the core derives a map only from valid mated GT records' actual `identity_id`/`fold` pairs, requiring consistent folds and coverage of every referenced probe/gallery identity. A complete valid context is accepted; a missing identity, contradictory mated GT folds or used test identity raises `CalibrationLeakError`. Do not blanket-refuse `None`, guess missing identities into fit, or derive provenance from predictions. Malformed map keys/fold values raise `FrameContractError`. No new error class or record field is added.
   - **Unit score.** Each unit's score is the maximum top-1 non-mated score over its records. For a non-mated GT face this is its one score. For a background unit it is the maximum over its detections.
   - **Selection rule (the only one).** Pool the calibration units. Match them on sex, age and origin where the frame allows (CAL-04). Background units have no demographic field and form their own stratum. Let N be the pooled unit count and m = floor(fpir_target × N).
     - `legacy_v1` (accept iff score > τ): τ is the (m+1)-th largest pooled unit score. It is the smallest τ with FPI ≤ m.
     - `v2` (accept iff score ≥ τ): let w be the (m+1)-th largest pooled unit score and u the smallest pooled unit score strictly greater than w. τ is the midpoint of w and u. If no pooled score is greater than w, τ is the next representable float above w. This keeps the cut strictly above w, so it stays feasible when scores tie.
     - If N = 0, raise `TauInfeasibleError`. Never return +inf or −inf.
   - Per-fold τ is computed the same way from each calibration fold of the primary rotation alone and reported as the spread (EXP-30). The primary test fold never enters it, and it never feeds the test read or changes the pooled τ (MCP 16810).
   - `scores.json` records the chosen τ, the pooled FPI at τ (unit count), and the FPI and FNIR at the next lower candidate threshold.
4. `fpir_resolvable(target, n_nonmated_eff)`: true iff n_nonmated_eff ≥ 3/target (300 at 0.01, 3,000 at 0.001). n_nonmated_eff counts non-mated source units divided by their PSU deff at `planning_icc` (S1 Build 7). This is the conservative floor; the exact zero-event floor at 0.01 is about 298. When unresolvable, report the measured FPIR with its upper limit as a designed unknown (CARD-03).
   - The FPIR upper limit uses the exact one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson bound on the integer FPI and the integer unit count N_units, labelled as an iid approximation. For the clustered pool, report the PSU-resampled percentile bound when FPI > 0. When FPI = 0, report the rule-of-three bound 3/n_nonmated_eff, labelled approximate.
   - Never pass a fractional n_eff as the binomial n.
   - Validate FPIR-UCL coverage separately from FNIR-delta coverage (zero events, unequal cluster sizes, weighted probability arm).
5. `paired_cluster_bootstrap(outcomes_a, outcomes_b, psu_of, *, n_boot, seed, level, weights=None) -> PairedInterval`:
   - Resample PSUs with replacement and recompute ΔFNIR = FNIR_a − FNIR_b on the same faces.
   - For verdict intervals, `decide()` sets the two-sided `level`: 0.95 for step 1, 0.90 for step 2. Callers cannot override that decision policy; descriptive cell/gap intervals use 0.95.
   - Return `PairedInterval(delta, lower, upper, n_psu, icc, level, n_boot, seed)`. `icc` is the observed ICC of the paired contrast, null for constant contrast or insufficient replication; no observed deff field is returned. It does not change n_eff, which uses the manifest ICC (S1).
   - No p-value and no test inversion.
   - Unit of resampling: PSU (image–identity component). Unit of estimand: mated GT faces, weighted per face. Interval: percentile, two-sided at the step level. The resample count and seed come from the manifest, `interval.n_boot` (10000) and `interval.seed` (2429382368; MCP 16733). The independent unit is the PSU, not the image (EXP-23).
   - The `probability` sampling arm weights each record by 1 / `inclusion_probability` (S1 Build 1; the IPW weight). The `challenge` arm is reported separately and never pooled; its weight is 1.
6. `decide(manifest, results) -> Verdict`. This is the only verdict rule. It uses confidence intervals only. It computes no p-values and does not call `holm_bonferroni`.
   - **Primary rotation (MCP 16810).** `decide()` receives results for the single primary rotation only: test fold `fold_protocol.seed % k`, with τ fit on its four complementary folds. Results from any other rotation are unsealed development diagnostics and never reach `decide()` as verdict input.
   - **Step order and levels.** The manifest's `hypotheses` list is the Holm step order: H1, then H2. K = 2, and `alpha_family` = 0.05 is one-sided. Step k tests at one-sided level α_family / (K − k + 1): step 1 = 0.025, step 2 = 0.05. The two-sided interval level is 1 − 2 × one-sided level: 0.95 for step 1, 0.90 for step 2.
   - **H1 (superiority)**, Δ = FNIR_SF − FNIR_AF: rejects iff the step-1 percentile lower bound is > 0 and every veto guardrail holds.
   - **H2 (non-inferiority)**, Δ = FNIR_leg − FNIR_IF: tested only if H1 rejected. Rejects iff the step-2 percentile upper bound is < δ (δ = 0.05) and every veto guardrail holds. The leg is the single in-house leg named in H2's `legs` in the sealed manifest. `decide()` raises `UndeclaredH2LegError` if `results` lack that leg or carry results built for any other in-house leg as H2.
   - **Stop rule.** Stop at the first non-rejection. A hypothesis that is not reached has no state. A non-rejection is `inconclusive`, whether the interval spans the boundary or lies entirely on the other side. The verdict prints the interval and the side.
   - **Check order.** Apply these checks in order and stop at the first that applies:
     1. Not ratified or not sealed: `unratified`, numbers only (`require_verdict_allowed`).
     2. Eligibility, before any interval is read:
        - Independent PSUs: the distinct PSUs over the frame's scored mated faces and non-mated source units must number at least `min_n_independent` (50). Fewer gives `inconclusive` with `insufficient_independent_psus` for every hypothesis, before the FPIR check and before any interval is read (MCP 16736; EXP-23). This global floor proves no adequacy. It does not replace power, MDE, FPIR resolvability or the exhaustive-GT requirement (MCP 16733).
        - Primary FPIR unresolvable (`fpir_resolvable` false at the frame's n_nonmated_eff): `inconclusive` with reason `primary_fpir_unresolvable`, applied to every hypothesis, and no winner is named regardless of the FNIR interval.
        - A veto guardrail is null or unscored, or a per-cohort FPIR has n_eff under 3/FPIR: `inconclusive` with reason `required_guardrail_unscored`. A resolvable per-cohort FPIR above its limit is a failure, not an unscored value.
     3. Veto: a veto guardrail with a measured value outside its limit gives `guardrail_vetoed` for the hypothesis being decided.
     4. The step-level tests above.
   - **MDE** (shown before unseal, for each step). MDE is the smallest Δ > 0 for which the scope §Power formula gives a required n ≤ n_eff. This computed minimum detectable effect describes design sensitivity at the given n_eff, discordance and target power. It is distinct from the predeclared practical margin / smallest effect size of interest (SESOI); H2's δ = 0.05 remains fixed before outcomes (EXP-12; CARD-27). Use the scope § Power formula `n_required(Δ) = [z·√p_d + z_β·√(p_d − Δ²)]² / Δ²`. Use z_{α/2} = 1.96 for H1 (one-sided 0.025) and 1.645 for H2 (one-sided 0.05), z_β = 0.8416 at manifest `power` = 0.80 (scope § Power, project policy), and manifest `p_d` = 0.15 (a design assumption, not observed). Read both required fields from the manifest with no code defaults (MCP 16756); derive z_β from that target power. The unit is effective mated searches (n_eff), computed from the actual mated queries only. The whole-inventory `Frame.n_eff` and the non-mated `n_nonmated_eff` are never substituted (MCP 16810). Solve by bisection; the required n is monotone in Δ. Write MDE to `scores.json`. If H2 is reached and its MDE > δ, H2 is `inconclusive` with reason `margin_unreachable_mde_gt_delta`, whatever its interval says.
   - **Verdict states.** Each evaluated hypothesis carries one `VerdictState`. H1 rejected gives `superior`. H2 rejected gives `non_inferior` only on a frame whose registry entry is `confirmatory`; on any other frame H2 is `inconclusive` with reason `non_confirmatory_frame`, and its numbers stay on the report. H1 may give `superior` on a development frame.
   - The `Verdict` stores the state per hypothesis id, the reason, the step levels, the interval and MDE for each step, and the decision path.
7. Pair cells:
   - Build the query occluder × enrolled occluder table with faces, identities, PSUs and FNIR per leg.
   - A cell is "not scored" below `cell_floor_n_independent` (30) independent PSUs (MCP 16733). This floor is separate from `min_n_independent` (50), which gates the frame in Build 6.
   - Real and synthetic cells are kept in separate tables. Cells with `unknown` origin are reported as `unknown` and are never merged into either table (MCP 16810).
   - Each typed row has `leg_key: string`, `query_occluder: string`, `enrolled_occluder: string`, `data_origin: real | synthetic | unknown`, `n_units: int`, `n_identities: int`, `n_psu: int`, `n_eff: number`, `scored: bool`, `fnir: number | null`, `interval: {lower: number, upper: number, level: number} | null`. Counts are nonnegative; `n_units` counts mated GT faces, not nonmated source units. Below the cell floor, counts remain and FNIR/interval are null. The global floor gates interval computation separately, so a scored rate may have null interval.
   - The actual cell estimand is this leg's single FNIR rate. IO `_interval` calls `paired_cluster_bootstrap` against an all-correct reference to obtain a 95% PSU percentile rate interval; this is not a between-leg paired difference. Use the manifest's full 10000 bootstraps/seed; serialize only lower/upper/level, without inventing a method field.
   - `data_origin` is real only with explicit real provenance, synthetic only with an explicit synthetic parent, otherwise unknown. Occluder sets serialize as sorted `+` composites; null becomes `unknown`, empty becomes `clear`. Enrolled occluders come only from `gallery_membership.enrolled_occluders`, never predictions.
8. Stage waterfall and joint gaps.
   - Counts per `SearchStage` per leg, over mated GT faces on the native arm. The fused leg has no `unaligned` count.
   - For each attribution arm (`gt_crop_redetect`, `cached_yunet_landmark_replay`), report the joint gap: ΔFNIR = FNIR(native) − FNIR(arm) over the mated faces where both the arm and native records are `available`, with its paired PSU interval. Faces with `arm_availability` `unavailable` or `unavailable_fused` are counted per arm and excluded from that gap. They are never imputed as misses.
   - No percentage shares are apportioned to localization, alignment or the embedder. No arm is named as a cause. The gap is a joint intervention effect.
   - Wire (actual IO/schema): `joint_gaps` is an array, unique by `(leg_key, arm)`, with typed fields `leg_key: string`, `arm: gt_crop_redetect | cached_yunet_landmark_replay`, `scored: bool`, `reason: string | null`, `n_pairs: int`, `n_psu: int`, `n_eff: number`, `delta: number | null`, `interval: {lower: number, upper: number, level: number} | null`, `unavailable_counts: {native: int, attribution: int}`. Counts are nonnegative; native unavailable and attribution missing/unavailable faces are counted separately and excluded from pairs, never imputed as misses. Scored rows have null reason, numeric delta and a paired 95% PSU interval; unscored rows keep counts/support and a named reason with null delta/interval.
9. Guardrails from the manifest. Each guardrail has a `role` set in the sealed manifest: `veto` gates the verdict; `report_only` is written to `scores.json` and the report and never gates.
   - per-cohort FPIR (each cohort with resolvability, over that cohort's non-mated source units; background units count only in the all-cohort FPIR) — veto
   - false names per 1k name mentions — veto
   - worst pair-cell FNIR, over the cells named in the guardrail's `cohort_scope`; unscored if any named cell is not scored — veto
   - p95 ms per GT face — veto (MCP 16856). Within native primary-test probability scope, `N_GT` is the independently exhaustive GT-face count. Each GT face on image i (`m_i > 0`) receives `image_total_i/m_i + sum(empty_GT_image_total)/N_GT`. Include ALL native image and record work (detection, alignment, embedding, search). Exactly `N_GT` samples conserve total cost; extra detections mint no samples, empty images no fake bins. At `N_GT = 0`, unscored. Use the linear 95th percentile in `ms/GT_face`. Raw `timing_receipt_ms` declares raw milliseconds explicitly; do not invent another mandatory receipt field. The executable rule must declare derived unit `ms/GT_face`; incomplete costs or an unsupported rule/unit is unscored (`required_guardrail_unscored` for a veto). Numeric ms remain RAM/receipt; semantic p95 `value` is null and only state/receipt SHA are stored.
   - Guardrail wire rows have `id: string`, `metric: string`, `role: veto | report_only`, `state: pass | fail | unscored`, `value: number | null`, `timings_sha256: string | null`. Optional typed `evidence` includes all fields `leg_key: string | null`, `cohort: string | null`, `n_units: int | null`, `n_psu: int | null`, `n_eff: number | null`, `limit: number | null`, `reason: string | null`; counts/support retain metric-specific units. For p95, evidence `limit` is null; no numeric milliseconds enter semantic evidence.
   - cost per accepted description (nullable column) — report-only until its source (S2 timings plus the retry and abstention ledger) is wired
10. Output files.
    - `scores.json` is the semantic assessment output, validated against the accepted per-leg `scores-v1` correction (S0 Build 9; README partial-freeze routing). It is deterministic and sorted for pinned assessment inputs, with no clock reads. It records τ and the calibration spread, the pooled FPI at τ, N_units, the FPI at the next lower candidate, the distinct PSU count with the floors used, `p_d`, `planning_icc`, n_eff, deff, MDE and step levels per step, each interval, the state and reason per hypothesis, and each guardrail value with its role. It also records the ICC sensitivity rows (n_eff, resolvability and MDE at planning ICC 0.05, 0.10 and 0.20; EXP-30; MCP 16733). The verdict uses only the manifest's `planning_icc`. For the p95 veto it records the state only (`pass`, `fail` or `unscored`) and the sha256 of the `timings.json` it read. The receipt SHA and veto state are assessment inputs, and report decisions transitively depend on that receipt through scores. Whole scores/report byte replay therefore requires all inputs pinned, including one identical timing receipt (MCP 16777; FLOW-18, **Make report recomputation deterministic or mark it non-replayable**, *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, ch. 10).
    - `timings.json` is written by the run (S6a), never by `score`. `score` reads it and never rewrites it. Receipt production is non-replayable. Fresh physical timings are new assessment inputs and may change the real veto, scores and report. Across cold runs, compare pure searches and recognition ledgers, keeping actual attempt receipts separate. For AE-7 full-score/report replay, use explicit verification snapshots with otherwise identical semantic inputs and one fixed receipt; preserve all original receipts. Never mask or normalize actual veto/hash fields or reread a spent sealed frame. No new CLI argument is needed (MCP 16777).
    - Required `decision_path: string[]` is `list(verdict.decision_path)` from the core, preserving actual order without fabricated, sorted or deduplicated steps. Empty traces are only for explicitly labelled legacy/no-trace fixtures. Typed arrays/wrappers are as in Builds 2, 7–9. The primary test-fold/calibration basis is persisted in frame/registry and calibration evidence; do not invent top-level score fields for it. Other rotations stay separate unsealed development diagnostics.
    - The landed IO writes `list(verdict.decision_path)`, includes empty-GT image costs in `timing_exposure`, and keeps numeric p95 `evidence.limit` null. These earlier integration gaps are corrected in source. Real receipt-dependent veto evidence and AE-12 acceptance remain separate requirements.
    - Optional `face_outcomes` is present in the current producer and optional in `scores-v1`; older valid scores may omit it. Each row has exactly `face_id`, `image_id`, `psu_id`, `fold`, `fold_role`, `sampling_arm`, `leg_key`, `arm`, `arm_availability`, `stage_reached`, `identity_status`, `mated`, `cell`, `outcome`, `outcome_available`, `outcome_reason`. Rows are sorted by `(leg_key, face_id)` and join every GT face to every declared leg/arm ledger, including upstream misses, unresolved identities and fit rows. Detector-only detections stay in raw ledgers/background accounting, outside this GT view.
    - `mated` is actual declared per-query-fold enrollment, false for resolved nonmates and null for unresolved identity. It is independent of fold role. The actual classifier supplies an available test outcome using the matching native frozen τ, including for attribution arms. Null outcomes have `outcome_available=false` and a named reason, in producer precedence: `fit_fold_not_scored`, `arm_unavailable`, `unresolved_identity`, `calibration_unavailable`, then `outcome_unavailable`. A fit row retains provenance with null outcome; an unresolved row never becomes a nonmate or miss. An available mated upstream miss remains `missed` in FNIR. Keep other eligible rows scored when one row is unresolved.

## RED tests

- Analytic fixture: 10 mated faces with known top-1 and 20 non-mated source units. FPI and N_units are exact integers, compared with `==` against hand counts. FNIR and FPIR are rate oracles computed with `fractions.Fraction` and compared with the float implementation within an absolute tolerance of 1e-12 (MCP 16736).
