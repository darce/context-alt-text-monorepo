## 4. Estimand

This candidate is an **H2-only opt-in**, prospective method, `paired_psu_studentized_h2_v2`. It is not an implemented API. H1, v1 replay, and every existing descriptive leg/cell interval retain their current percentile method. Selecting H2's method must not change the shared percentile function's default or dispatch all its callers to studentization.

- Face i in paired mated GT PSU j(i) has finite weight wᵢ > 0. Define mᴬᵢ = 1 for an outcome other than `correct`, else 0, and likewise mᴮᵢ. Then Yᵢ = mᴬᵢ − mᴮᵢ ∈ {−1, 0, 1}.
- **Legs.** Leg A is the in-house leg named by the manifest's sealed H2 non-inferiority hypothesis: `auraface` or `sface`. Those are the two members of the current `_IN_HOUSE_LEGS` (`firbench_contract.py:291–294`). `_check_h2_leg` (`firbench_score.py:827–841`) requires exactly one in-house leg in that hypothesis and requires `LegResults.h2_leg` to match it. Leg B is the reference `insightface`. The leg labels A and B are distinct from the bootstrap draw count B in §§7–11. H2's estimand is Δ = E[S_j] / E[M_j], where S_j = Σ_{i∈j} wᵢYᵢ and M_j = Σ_{i∈j} wᵢ. This is a face-weighted ratio under the declared PSU sampling design, not an equally weighted average of PSU rates. Harm is Δ ≥ 0.05: the selected in-house leg misses, face-weighted, at least 0.05 more often than insightface. The dominant error is a false non-inferiority claim.
- **Separately sealed routes, one method contract.** The auraface-selected and sface-selected routes are both legal under the same H2-only method ID, configuration identity and frozen method specification. The leg is a manifest input, not a method parameter, score-time option or leg-specific variant of SE, quantile, B or seed. Each prospective experiment declares exactly one selected leg in its own reviewed manifest. Before any outcome is generated or inspected, it freezes that leg with the method, margin, harm direction, α and gates. Each route therefore has its own Δ and its own seal. §10.2 labels the routes A (auraface selected) and S (sface selected); in either route, the selected leg plays the role of leg A here. Neither route's outcomes may choose, replace, pool with or rescue the other. Running both routes in calibration is a conformance obligation, not a menu for reporting the better leg, and it creates no new α budget (EXP-22, **No circular analysis**, *Statistics Done Wrong*, ch. 6; CARD-27). The post-run selection control in the S9 brief stays a labelled INVALID negative control, never a selection route.
- **Historical attribution.** The historical v1 manifest recorded by the S9 brief declares H2 `[auraface, insightface]`, and the existing boundary suite selects auraface (§1). The v6 H2 boundary result, 0.063 > 0.060 and FAILED, is attributed to that auraface-selected route. That attribution describes the original evidence only. It neither restricts the proposed API to auraface nor transfers to an sface-route result, and no later result rescues it. A new experiment with either leg needs its own reviewed freeze. The default `fir-bench-decision-v1.json` and its auraface declaration stay unchanged.

## 7. Estimator formulas and deterministic arithmetic (prospective)

These mathematical conventions apply only to the opt-in H2 method. They are requirements for a future implementation, not claims about current APIs. §8's eligibility and paired-K checks precede both observed and replicate SE computation.

**Ratio derivation, separate from the regression sources.** For K ≥ 2 paired PSUs, let S = Σ_j S_j, M = Σ_j M_j > 0, and δ̂ = S/M. The estimating equation is Σ_j(S_j − δ̂M_j) = 0. For independent identically distributed PSU totals, linearization of the ratio gives influence (S_j − ΔM_j)/E[M_j]. Estimating the variance of the mean residual with sample variance denominator K − 1, and replacing E[M_j] by M/K, yields

V̂ = [K/(K−1)] Σ_j [(S_j − δ̂M_j)/M]²; SE_hat = √V̂.

This is a first-order plug-in variance estimate under §4's conditions, not a finite-sample guarantee or a formula certified by either paper. Planning ICC and n_eff still own sizing/MDE; neither is substituted for K in this variance.

1. **Safe observed arithmetic.** Normalize before squaring: a_j = S_j/M, p_j = M_j/M, δ̂ = Σ_j a_j, r_j = a_j − δ̂p_j, V̂ = [K/(K−1)] Σ_j r_j². All totals, divisions and residuals have the exact-real meaning specified below. Never square unnormalized S_j, M_j or their totals in fixed-range float arithmetic. Positive denominators and |S_j| ≤ M_j must be verified; a violated invariant is a numerical failure, not a zero denominator or a corrected/clipped statistic.
2. **Exactly B index draws.** Draw K indices independently and uniformly with replacement from these K PSUs for each b = 1..B, retaining the source's PSU ordering and index-gather/RNG consumption convention. Every selected occurrence is a separate resampled PSU, including repeated selections of the same original PSU. Do not collapse duplicates and then replace K by the number of unique selected originals.
3. **Replicate normalization.** M*_b = Σ_{k=1}^K M_{j_k}, S*_b = Σ_{k=1}^K S_{j_k}, δ*_b = S*_b/M*_b. Set a*_{bk} = S_{j_k}/M*_b, p*_{bk} = M_{j_k}/M*_b, r*_{bk} = a*_{bk} − δ*_bp*_{bk}. Then V*_b = [K/(K−1)] Σ_k (r*_{bk})² and SE*_b = √V*_b. Observed and replicate arithmetic use the same policy; merely extending total accumulation while leaving squares in float64 is insufficient.
4. **Studentization.** For V*_b > 0 use t*_b = (δ*_b − δ̂)/√V*_b. For exact V*_b = 0 use §8's declared extended-real conventions. A very small positive variance remains positive: it is never replaced by zero, an epsilon or a floor.
5. **Quantiles over all B.** Sort the entire extended-real multiset (−∞ first, +∞ last). For 0 < q < 1 define Q(q) as the one-indexed value at rank ceil(qB), with no interpolation, filtering or B+1 adjustment. Use exact rational q and integer rank arithmetic: at the H2 level 0.90, q is 1/20 or 19/20. B, seed and PSU ordering remain frozen inputs; this does not change the v1 linear percentile quantile. The rank convention is a protocol choice, not a prescription from the papers.

**Precision and zero policy.** The normative reference treats each accepted binary64 face weight as its exact dyadic rational value, with arbitrary-size integer/rational accumulation of S_j, M_j, observed and replicate statistics and V. **Recoverable arithmetic failure versus process interruption.** A *recoverable arithmetic failure* is a condition the pinned reference or certified implementation detects and returns as a value: a violated numeric invariant (for example |S_j| > M_j or a zero replicate denominator), failure of the frozen certification procedure to decide a zero classification, sign, rank order or final rounding, or a required final scalar that §7 does not allow to be emitted. It is a deterministic function of the sealed inputs, method specification and pinned implementation, so replay reproduces it. A *process interruption* is anything that prevents the kernel from returning its typed result: an uncaught exception or implementation defect, memory or other resource exhaustion, a signal, timeout, kill or host loss. Environment- or resource-dependent conditions are always interruptions, because they do not replay deterministically. §7's "exact evaluation/certification cannot complete" is read here as the frozen procedure terminating deterministically without a decision. Exhausting memory, time or another resource before returning is an interruption. If §7's owner intends otherwise, that conflict blocks freeze. The kernel must not catch interruptions and relabel them `h2_studentized_numeric_failure`, and a recoverable failure must not be escalated into an abort. An interruption has no reason, interval, quantile, rejection or pass; §§10.5 and 11 record it as incomplete.

Their margins and the t* categories (finite-negative; finite-zero = both zero-numerator categories; finite-positive; −∞; +∞) are derived from these counts, not stored separately.
